We are building an agentic AI system that can analyze system and application logs and metrics for arbitrary unknown systems and applications. We will call it 'beeper' 
We need beeper to detect faults, plan remediation, perform remediation, then document conditions and remediation steps into a wiki-style data store.
We need that data store to allow human engineers to approve, revise, and enter their own advice including any known conditions and remediations.
