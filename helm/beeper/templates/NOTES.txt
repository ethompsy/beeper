PLATFORM RESILIENCE (NFR14 / NFR17):
  Beeper enhances your existing alerting — it never replaces it.
  If the Beeper operator is unavailable, your existing Prometheus and
  Loki alerting pipeline continues functioning normally.

  Investigation data persists in Qdrant via persistent volumes and
  survives operator restarts without data loss.

LLM DEGRADATION (NFR15):
  If the LLM provider becomes unavailable, investigations are queued
  for retry with exponential backoff. A structured escalation event is
  emitted within 60 seconds for human awareness.

GRACEFUL SHUTDOWN:
  The operator waits up to 10 seconds for in-flight SLO engine cycles
  and detection processing to complete before terminating.

To check the operator status:
  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "beeper.name" . }}

To view active investigations:
  kubectl get investigations -A
